A 7-day trial from your first launch

All features are available for 7 days (168 hours) from your first app launch. This trial is provided inside the app. Ending the trial does not subscribe or charge you automatically. To keep using Pro, you must choose to purchase it through the store.

Trial information is stored on your device. This is an installation-based trial with no app account or server. A once-per-person trial across reinstalls or device changes is not guaranteed.

Features after the trial

Always free

  • Calendar and upcoming tasks
  • Create, edit, and delete tasks and notes
  • Repeating tasks, completion, and undo completion
  • View tasks in your home screen widget

첫칸 Pro

  • Scheduled and overdue reminders
  • Snooze and follow-up reminders
  • Start now and 5-minute starts

Pro features lock when the trial or subscription access period ends. Existing tasks and records are not automatically deleted, and free features remain available. Viewing tasks in the widget is free; starting or snoozing from it requires trial or Pro access.

Yearly Pro subscription with automatic renewal

Pro is an optional subscription billed once a year. It renews each year under the store renewal terms until you cancel. The actual price, currency, billing time, and conditions are shown by the store in the app and on the purchase confirmation screen.

Apple or Google processes payment. A purchase pending approval does not itself grant paid Pro access. Purchases and subscription status checks require communication with the store.

Confirmed paid access is not stopped merely because you leave the app closed for a few days or temporarily lose connectivity. A confirmed expiration date is also applied on your device. If the store does not provide an expiration date, status is checked at the next successful store query. Expiration or refund changes may take longer to reflect while offline.

Manage renewal, cancel, or request a refund

Use Manage subscription in the app or the subscription settings in the store where you subscribed. After canceling renewal, Pro remains available for the period you already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refund eligibility, procedures, and approval follow the store policy and applicable law. Canceling renewal and requesting a refund are separate actions.

Restoring purchases and keeping data

Use Restore purchases with the same store account on the platform where you purchased Pro to check a valid purchase. Purchases are not shared between iOS and Android.

Restoring purchases does not restore tasks, notes, or activity history. App data is stored on your device. There is no developer server, app account, cloud sync, or separate data recovery service. Uninstalling the app, clearing data, or changing devices may result in lost records.

Using the app

The basic calendar and notes work offline. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and does not send your tasks or activity history to a developer server.

Notification delivery and widget updates depend on operating system permissions, Focus and battery-saving modes, background execution, and the scheduling horizon. Delivery can be delayed or limited. Reopen the app to review scheduled reminders after changing settings or leaving it unused for a long time.
