Privacy in Todoniq

Your records stay on your device

This page explains the separate handling of app records and backups, files you choose to export, store purchases, support emails, and visits to this help website.

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Information stored by the app

Task titles, notes, small first steps, categories, dates and times, priority, repeating and reminder settings, and task status are stored in a local database on your device. Completion and snooze history are used to display tasks and calculate statistics.

Trial start and end times, app settings, and subscription status checked with the store are also stored on your device. The home screen widget uses a display snapshot of tasks stored on the same device. Starting or snoozing a task from the widget updates that task in the app and requires trial or Pro access.

These tasks and records are not sent to a developer server. The app has no registration, app account, operating server, advertising, analytics SDK, or cloud sync.

Local backup, file export, and restore

Backups contain tasks, notes, schedules, repeating settings, completion and activity history, and language and notification title preferences. Purchases, subscription status, and trial start and end times are excluded.

Manual and optional automatic backups keep one latest file in the app's local storage. Enabling automatic backup creates an initial backup. After 7 days, it replaces that file when you open the app or the operating system allows background work. Exact timing is not guaranteed, and the file's size can change as your records grow.

Automatic backups are not uploaded to a developer server or cloud service. Selecting Export backup file saves a JSON file to a location you choose in the device's file picker. If you choose a cloud provider's location, that provider's policies govern its file storage and transfer. The developer does not automatically receive the file.

Exported backups are not encrypted. Anyone with access to the file can read its tasks and notes. Check where you save it and whom you share it with, and delete unneeded copies from their storage location.

After your confirmation, restoring a backup replaces current tasks and activity history with the backup contents. Subscription and trial status stay unchanged. Whole-device backup and migration are separate operating system features; recovery of app records through those features is not guaranteed.

Purchases handled by the App Store

Buying Pro, restoring purchases, and checking subscription status require communication with the App Store. Payment is handled through your Apple Account and the App Store payment system. The app checks product prices, purchases, and subscription status.

The developer does not directly collect card details or store account passwords. The store privacy policy applies to its payment processing.

Apple Privacy Policy

Expiration or refund status may take longer to reflect while offline. The basic calendar and notes work without internet access.

Notification permission

Notification permission is used to show scheduled tasks and follow-up reminders on your device. Denied permissions or device restrictions can prevent or delay delivery.

Task titles are shown in notifications by default. You can turn notification titles off in the app settings to show the app name and a general message instead. This preference is stored on the device. It does not hide titles in the app or widgets, or delete the records themselves.

You can change notification permission, previews, and sound in your device settings. Changing permission does not automatically delete stored tasks or notes.

Keeping and deleting local records

Deleting a task in the app removes tasks and records within the scope you choose from the current database. Ending a trial or subscription does not automatically delete existing tasks or activity history.

A backup made before deletion may still contain those records. Creating a new local backup replaces the previous local copy. Files you exported must be managed or deleted separately at the location where you saved them.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data can also remove local records and backups, and they may not transfer automatically to a new device. Export records you need and keep the file separately. The developer does not keep your records on a server or provide a separate recovery service.

Store purchase restoration checks access to Pro. It does not restore deleted tasks or notes. If you saved a backup, you can restore it through the app's backup and restore feature.

Support emails you choose to send

Contact dabok407@gmail.com. Sending a message from your mail app provides information separately from local app storage. The app does not automatically send an email or attach your tasks.

The email address, name, message, and attachments you provide are handled to review and answer your request and provide necessary follow-up. Your chosen email service and the receiving Gmail service are involved in delivering and storing messages.

Information needed for an ongoing request is kept while the request is being handled. Information no longer needed for the response or follow-up is deleted. Any information that must be retained by law is kept for the required scope and period. Send requests to access, correct, or delete support information to the same email address.

We do not request passwords, verification codes, API keys, card details, or sensitive tasks or notes. Do not send unnecessary personal information, and hide personal details in screenshots.

This help website

This static help website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not add advertising or analytics tools, a support submission form, or external fonts to the site.

Visiting this website does not upload your app tasks or notes. Website connection information and local app data are handled separately.
