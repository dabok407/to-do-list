If a reminder does not arrive

  1. Check that notifications for 첫칸 are allowed in your device settings.
  2. Check Focus or battery-saving settings. On Android, also check the exact alarm permission.
  3. Try Test reminder in 10 seconds in the app settings. Reminders require an active trial or Pro access.
  4. Open the app and review the task time and overdue reminder settings.

Reminders are scheduled on your device, so you do not need to open the app for every reminder. Device permissions, battery policies, a force stop, and the scheduling horizon can delay or limit delivery and widget updates. Open the app again after changing settings or leaving it unused for a long time to refresh scheduled reminders.

Korean and English

In the app settings, choose Language, then System, 한국어, or English. Interface and reminder text follow your choice. Titles and notes you enter are never translated or changed.

Restore purchases and local records

Use Restore purchases with the same store account on the platform where you purchased Pro to check a valid purchase. Purchases, restoration, and subscription checks require communication with Apple or Google. Purchases are not shared between iOS and Android.

Restoring a purchase does not restore tasks, notes, or activity history. Records are stored on your device. There is no app account, cloud sync, or separate data recovery service. Uninstalling the app, clearing device data, or changing devices may result in lost records.

Cancel a subscription or request a refund

Use Manage subscription in the app or the subscription settings in the store where you subscribed to manage renewal. After canceling renewal, Pro remains available for the period you already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
